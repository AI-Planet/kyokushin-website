What Is Kyokushin?

Kyokushin is a full-contact style of karate founded by Masutatsu Oyama. It is known for demanding training, physical conditioning, powerful techniques and the pursuit of personal development.

Keep going, even when the challenge is difficult.

Kyokushin training aims to develop not only physical ability, but also perseverance, self-control, respect and determination.

History of Kyokushin

Kyokushin was established by Masutatsu Oyama in the 1960s. The style became known internationally for its demanding training, full-contact fighting and emphasis on mental and physical development. Its practitioners have helped spread the art across many countries.

Different organisations have developed within the Kyokushin tradition. Their histories, leadership and grading systems may differ.
